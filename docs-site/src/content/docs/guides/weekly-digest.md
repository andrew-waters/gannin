The **Weekly Digest** button in the bar on PR flow writes up the week: delivery against the week before and your goals, what shipped by investment category, notable PRs, CI, and who's off next week.

In the sheet you can:

- **Copy** it.
- **Rewrite with Claude** to change its tone or shape.
- **Commit to Harness** to save it as a file in your project's harness, under `digests/` with the date. The button says where it will go, and nothing is committed until you press it.

## Standup notes

Standup has a **Notes** button too (disabled in the Changelog layout). It writes the day as notes per person, with the same choices: copy, rewrite with Claude, or commit to the harness under `standups/`. See [Standup](/docs/guides/standup/).
