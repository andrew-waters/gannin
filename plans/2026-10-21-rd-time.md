# R&D time

## Approach

Every step is a strategy kept in the project's `.gannin/rnd.json` (`RDConfig`), so a team can pick how it works and later versions can add strategies without changing the report.

- **What counts** (`RDQualification`): issues in chosen investment categories (so rule-based marking comes from the categories' rules), issues with any of a list of labels, or issues with chosen options of a board's single-select field. Any issue can be marked by hand on top (`RDMark`): counted or not, the share of it that's R&D (partial allocation) and a rationale. Sub-issues inherit from their parent unless marked themselves.
- **How time is found** (`RDTimeSource`, an ordered list): the first version has From activity only. Each working day's hours (the person's pattern or the org's week, less bank holidays and leave from Time off) are split across the issues they touched that day: commits and reviews on PRs that close an issue, comments on an issue. Evenly or by events (a setting); days with no activity go to the issues behind their open PRs, or stay unallocated (a setting). Entered by hand, From estimate and From sessions are later sources; entered time's storage (this Mac or the harness) comes with it, since activity-derived time stores nothing per person.
- **Period**: the accounting year's start (month and day), with this and last accounting year, this and last quarter, and a custom range.
- **Wording**: the name ("R&D") is a setting. No money, rates or scheme rules anywhere.
- Every allocation keeps its source and the events behind it, so each line traces back to the issue and its PRs.

Answers taken when planning: storage of entered time configurable (lands with that source), From activity as the first source, the page under Delivery.
