The problem

Agents write code at one speed. Everything around it runs at another.

Coding agents can turn an issue into a pull request in an afternoon. But knowing who's overloaded, which reviews are stuck, whether the board matches the code, and what shipped this week still means clicking through GitHub, boards, spreadsheets and chat.

Gannin puts the team's work, its delivery and its agents in the same window, so the bottleneck is never the part nobody can see.

What Gannin does

See the work. Ship it with agents. Plan with the whole picture.

See who's doing what

Every open PR, review request and assigned issue, per person and per team, across your orgs. An Inbox for your own queue, a standup for yesterday, time off on the calendar.

Ship with Claude Code

Work on This turns an issue into a Claude Code session, tabbed beside the others, with a live diff, its questions answered in a click, and its PRs and checks watched for you.

Plan with context

Plans, requirements and findings in your team's harness repo, linked to the issues they're about. Plan with Claude, break a requirement into issues, and keep the board in step.

Agents

Run five agents without losing the thread

Each issue gets its own Claude Code session in a tab. Gannin tells you which one needs you, shows the question with its options, keeps a live diff of every worktree, and resumes the conversation after a restart.

  • Questions and permission prompts answered in a click
  • Comment on the diff and send it back to Claude
  • Failing checks and review feedback flagged as they land

Delivery

Know why the numbers moved

Cycle time split into coding, waiting, rework and merging, by month, quarter or year, against the period before and your goals. Gannin says which stages, repos and people moved it, and writes the weekly digest.

  • Goals per team, on track or not
  • PR size, and large PRs rushed through review
  • Weekly digest, ready to share or commit
Review

Review faster, without handing over the decision

When a review lands on you, start Claude on it from the notification or the menu bar. Its findings sit on the PR's diff by severity. Keep, edit or dismiss each, add your own, and post one review to GitHub.

  • Review requests checked every few minutes
  • Suggestions posted as one-click GitHub suggestions
  • Nothing posted until you say so

How it works

Up and running before standup

01

Sign in with GitHub

Pick the orgs you look after. Gannin reads your PRs, issues, boards and Actions; everything you enter syncs through your own iCloud.

02

Point it at your harness

Choose the repo where plans and standards live, or create one. The team's settings can live there too, reviewed like code.

03

Work on an issue

Click Work on This. Claude Code starts in the harness with the issue's brief, on your Mac or your server, signed in as you.
